Approach

Every project starts with direction, not generation.

We shoot the product ourselves, build the atmosphere around it, and bring in AI only where it earns its place — for light, for mist, for space. Never for the product itself.

The work is composed, graded, and finished by hand before it ever reaches a feed.

AI is a tool. Not the author.

About

YK AI Studio is a small creative studio based in Japan.

Founded and directed by Yuki, the studio focuses on a narrow craft: quiet, well‑composed visual stories for fragrance, beauty, and lifestyle brands — made with real product photography and AI‑assisted production.

Small by design. Built to grow one project at a time.
